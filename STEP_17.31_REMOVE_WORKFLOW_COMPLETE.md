## Overview
Menghapus **semua visualisasi workflow/process** dari Skills & Tools section karena sudah ada **dedicated Design Process section** di tempat lain dalam portfolio.

---

## 🎯 Tujuan

### Masalah yang Diselesaikan
**Redundancy**: Portfolio memiliki 2 tempat yang menjelaskan process/workflow
1. ❌ ~~Skills & Tools → Design Workflow (kompak)~~
2. ✅ Design Process Section → Detailed process explanation

**Keputusan**: Hanya **satu** dedicated process section yang dibutuhkan.

### Positioning yang Benar
**Untuk UI/UX / Visual Design Internship:**
- ✅ "I have a solid design foundation and real project experience"
- ✅ "I use a practical design approach"
- ✅ "I'm continuing to learn and grow"

**Bukan:**
- ❌ "I already have a complete professional methodology"
- ❌ "I don't need to learn anything"
- ❌ "I have industry-proven process frameworks"

## 💡 Design Philosophy

### Skills & Tools Purpose
**SHOULD show:**
- ✅ What I can do (Design capabilities)
- ✅ What I use (Figma primary, Canva supporting)
- ✅ What supports my design (Technical knowledge)

**SHOULD NOT show:**
- ❌ Detailed process/methodology
- ❌ Step-by-step workflow
- ❌ Design thinking frameworks
- ❌ Process stages

**SHOULD show:**
- ✅ How I approach projects
- ✅ Design process stages
- ✅ Detailed methodology
- ✅ Problem-solving approach
